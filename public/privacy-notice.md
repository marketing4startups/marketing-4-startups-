# Privacy and data

Marketing4Startups is a browser-based marketing planner. It does not ask for an account, sign-in, email address, or customer contact details.

## What the planner stores

Answers are autosaved in local storage in the browser on the device you are using. They are not sent to the app operator, Supabase, or an analytics service. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see the saved draft.

Use **Clear draft** to remove the planner's saved answers from this browser. Clearing site data in your browser also removes them. Downloaded PDF files are saved wherever you choose and are no longer controlled by the planner.

## Please avoid sensitive details

Do not enter passwords, payment details, health information, or unnecessary names and contact details about customers. Describe customer problems without identifying people.

## Website hosting

When the planner is hosted online, the web hosting provider may process basic connection data such as IP address and request logs under its own terms. The operator must add its legal identity, privacy contact, hosting details, retention practices, and applicable legal basis before using this as a public service. This notice is a prototype and does not establish GDPR compliance.
